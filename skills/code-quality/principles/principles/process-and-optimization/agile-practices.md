## What It Means

Prefer short feedback loops, iterative delivery, and adaptation to validated learning.

## Apply When

- Planning work across iterations with uncertain requirements.
- Balancing near-term delivery against future extensibility.

## Good vs Bad

- Good: add just enough structure to support next iterations with evidence.
- Bad: over-planning distant scenarios that displace current value.

## Tradeoffs and Conflicts

- Can conflict with `yagni` when teams over-interpret agility as prediction-heavy architecture.
- Tie-break default: in uncertain contexts, keep implementation minimal and defer speculative framework work.
