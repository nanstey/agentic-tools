# Before-After-Bridge (BAB)

## Core Contract

Tell a results story as the prior state, the improved state, then the path between them.
A drafting order, not a visible template: apply `terse` and `deslop` so it reads as a narrative, not labelled sections. Follow `CLAUDE.md` / `AGENTS.md` on conflict.

## Structure

1. **Before** — the situation before the change: the pain, cost, or limitation.
2. **After** — the situation now: the outcome, ideally with a concrete measure.
3. **Bridge** — how it was achieved: the approach, key challenges, and lessons, delivered once the audience is invested.

## When to Use

- Demos, launch write-ups, and presentations where impact should precede mechanism.
- PR summaries and screenshots that show a before/after and then explain the work.
- Pairs with `bluf`: the After is the bottom line; the Bridge is the earned detail.

## Guardrails

- Never label the parts (`Before:` / `After:` / `Bridge:`) in shipped prose; the narrative arc carries them.
- Make Before and After a true, comparable pair — same subject, same framing, real states, never a staged contrast.
- Quantify the After when a metric exists; a concrete result is what earns attention for the Bridge.
- Keep the Bridge proportional; deliver the how after the impact lands, not as an upfront methodology dump.

## Output Style

Prior pain, the improved state with a concrete measure, then the approach and lessons that got there.
